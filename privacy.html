Who we are

BIZXRAY (bizxray.co.uk), registered with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO registration reference: 00014479577). We are the data controller for the information described below.

What we collect

When you use the free diagnostic on this site, we process: the business name and website URL you enter (which may be your own business or one you're researching), and — if you choose to unlock the full report — your first name and email address.

Why we collect it

We use the business name/URL and any context you provide solely to generate your report via automated research; this data is not retained beyond 24 hours. We use your name and email, provided on a consent basis when you unlock a report, to send you the report and to get in touch about our diagnostic reports and related services. You can withdraw consent at any time (see below).

Lawful basis

Consent (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(a)) for the name/email collected at the unlock step. Legitimate interests for the transient processing of the business/URL used only to generate the report itself.

Who we share it with

Report generation uses Anthropic's Claude API (a US-based processor) to research and draft report content. Contact details are stored in our CRM, HubSpot. Both operate under standard contractual safeguards for international data transfers.

How long we keep it

Report content is deleted automatically after 24 hours. Contact details are retained in our CRM until you ask us to delete them or unsubscribe.

Your rights

To exercise any of these, or to raise a complaint directly with us, contact: stephen@integratingsolutions.org. We will acknowledge any complaint within 30 days.
